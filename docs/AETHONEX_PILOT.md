# Aethonex Pilot

## Pilot offer

- initial test price: £2,500 UK / $3,000 US; not validated by completed customer purchases
- target duration: 30 days, subject to feasibility
- scope: one bounded workflow in a customer-controlled environment, one supported CSV input, one implemented prioritisation path, one human approval gate, and one already-supported and tested action provider or type
- planning limits: up to 200 target accounts and five users, subject to feasibility
- included handover: documentation, handover, agreed acceptance criteria, and provenance, action-result, or audit functionality where supported
- written agreement: confirms feasibility, final scope, exclusions, and payment terms before work begins

## Included

- deployment into a customer-controlled environment after technical and security approval
- environment configuration and validation, as scoped
- workflow implementation for the approved and supported CSV pilot path
- operator guidance and handover, as listed in the signed scope

## Not included

- universal enterprise rollout
- subscription platform
- broad self-service provisioning
- promises of business outcome without a real trial and agreed scope
- customer-data processing before signed data-processing terms and subprocessor review
- untested or unsupported integrations, additional action providers, and work outside the agreed scope
