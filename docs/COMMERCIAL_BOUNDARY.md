# Commercial Boundary

## Open Source Core

Free and open:

- workflow foundation
- deployment scripts
- validation logic
- Postgres-backed pilot path
- demo experience
- local runtime and docs

## Aethonex Services

Customers pay Aethonex for:

- deployment into a real customer environment
- DNS/TLS configuration
- secret management and environment hardening
- workflow implementation around the customer process
- operational support and updates
- data source integration and custom tuning
- pilot delivery and handover

## Future Commercial Modules

Potential premium modules may include:

- advanced connectors
- specialized workflow packs
- additional governance or approval patterns
- monitoring and support tiers
- operational runbooks tailored to customer environments

These are future opportunities, not hidden paid features inside the core.
