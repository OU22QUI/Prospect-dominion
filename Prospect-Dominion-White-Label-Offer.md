# Prospect Dominion — White-Label Offer

## Product positioning

Prospect Dominion can support a white-label deployment model for partners or customer-branded environments. This is not positioned as a speculative full multi-tenant SaaS promise. It is a partner-ready deployment architecture built for controlled branding and deployment within a supported operational model.

## What is branded

- customer or partner UI identity
- environment naming and public presentation
- configured product branding and rollout language
- customer-specific presentation layer

## What Aethonex provides

- underlying product platform
- deployment guidance
- configuration and validation support
- operational knowledge transfer
- product updates and baseline support

## What the partner controls

- deployment environment ownership
- branding decisions within supported boundaries
- internal end-user operational model
- customer access policies and rollout planning

## Setup model

A white-label deployment should be structured as a managed implementation with clear boundaries:
- brand and environment setup
- workflow configuration
- validation and deployment support
- ongoing maintenance and operational review

## Commercial framing

White-label should be sold as a partner-ready operating layer, not as an unbounded generic software platform. The message is: deploy the product under your brand, operate with governance, and keep the underlying platform reliable and supported.

## Key limitation

The white-label path should not imply unsupported full multi-tenant SaaS flexibility without a separate product and infrastructure plan. It is a branded deployment model, not an undocumented platform claim.
