# Prospect Dominion — Pricing Overview

## Executive summary

Prospect Dominion should be sold as a controlled, high-trust revenue execution platform with a clear pilot and deployment path. Exact public pricing should not be overexposed until there is enough commercial evidence to support it confidently.

The best public posture is:
- request pricing on the public site,
- use a pilot path as the first commercial conversion,
- use a deployment quote as the next step,
- reserve white-label pricing for partner-specific assessment.

## Recommended commercial structure

### 1. Pilot

Recommended public framing:
- starting range: $4,500-$12,000
- structure: time-boxed evaluation, limited scope, focused outcomes

What it includes:
- deployment configuration and environment setup
- basic product onboarding and workflow review
- limited usage for a defined team or account set
- success check-in and pilot review
- technical and commercial guidance during evaluation

What it excludes:
- custom production integrations outside scope
- broad white-label design work
- long-term support commitments beyond the pilot window
- custom feature development outside agreed scope

Rationale:
- lowers friction for early buying motion
- creates a clear path to a deployment conversion
- keeps technical risk manageable

Confidence:
- moderate; appropriate for early-stage validation but should be adjusted based on customer size and complexity

Evidence to raise price:
- high customer complexity
- more than one environment or multiple stakeholders
- significant custom workflow design
- extended support or board-level review needs

Evidence to reduce friction:
- small team or narrow use case
- low implementation complexity
- limited environment footprint

### 2. Deployment

Recommended public framing:
- starting range: $12,000-$35,000 setup + recurring support
- matching: client size, environment, scope, and workflow complexity

What it includes:
- customer deployment setup
- system configuration and branding
- workflow setup and validation
- technical deployment guidance and operational review
- documentation and operational handoff

What it excludes:
- unbounded custom feature development
- broad ongoing data engineering beyond agreed scope
- unsupported managed SaaS obligations unless separately contracted

Rationale:
- reflects the real cost of deployment and configuration work
- avoids over-selling a generic SaaS model before there is proven usage

Confidence:
- moderate; should be refined through real customer pilots and implementation learning

### 3. White-label

Recommended public framing:
- starting range: $25,000-$80,000 setup + recurring support and hosting alignment

What it includes:
- partner-branded deployment configuration
- environment-level branding and UI customization within supported boundaries
- assisted deployment setup and validation
- operational model guidance and partner onboarding

What it excludes:
- full multi-tenant SaaS platform development or infrastructure abstraction not yet in place
- unsupported large customizations beyond the current platform boundary

Rationale:
- the product is not positioned as a full self-service multi-tenant platform yet
- partner distribution is treated as a deployment model, not a large arbitrary SaaS promise

Confidence:
- moderate to low until a real white-label deployment establishes evidence

### 4. Custom

Use a custom quote for:
- large implementation scope
- multiple customer environments
- custom workflow design
- advanced partner or channel packaging

## Pricing presentation recommendation

Use request-pricing rather than exact public pricing in the public demo and website. This is the right commercial posture because:
- the product is still in real-world validation stage,
- pilot evidence is the most credible early driver of price,
- the product is still evolving in deployment and white-label patterns.

## Commercial guidance

The pricing should make the next step easy:
- demo → pilot → deployment → expansion → white-label

This preserves the sales motion while allowing adjustment based on real outcomes.
