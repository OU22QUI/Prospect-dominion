The problem

Signals live in one tool. Accounts in another. Relationships in people's heads. Actions in a sequencer. Outcomes nowhere.

Teams already have CRM, enrichment, sequencers, and alerts. The gap is the handoff: who notices the signal, checks context, chooses the action, and gets it approved before committing.

How Dominion works

Capture → Prioritise → Route → Govern

01

Capture

Connect the buying signals your team already collects from selected sources.

02

Prioritise

Apply prioritisation rules for fit, urgency, and available context.

03

Route

Recommend the next action and the person responsible for it.

04

Govern

Keep the workflow aligned with human approval rules and operator oversight before anything is sent.
