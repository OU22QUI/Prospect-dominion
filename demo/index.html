The problem

The signal is real. What happens next is not.

Teams already have a CRM, enrichment, a sequencer, and alerts. The gap is the handoff: who notices the signal, checks context, chooses the action, and gets it approved.

Signal to action

Connect the tools you already keep.

The pilot connects selected sources to a prioritised account queue and the next action your team actually runs.

Governed

Recommended actions stay visible and reviewable. A pilot defines the approval rule for the workflow that matters to you.

Owned
