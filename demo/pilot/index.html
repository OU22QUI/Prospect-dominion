The 30-Day Pilot Breakdown

What exactly do you get for $4,500?

A 30-day, fixed-price deployment of one signal-to-action workflow inside your own environment. No revenue guarantees, no software lock-in — yours to keep forever.

Question 01

What workflow do we choose?

You select one high-impact, high-friction signal-to-action loop that currently breaks between tools. Popular examples: intent signal to warm intro, churn risk to account review, or partner overlap to co-sell outreach.

Question 02

What gets built?

We deploy a single-tenant Dominion baseline, connect 1–2 data sources (e.g. CRM, intent provider, or spreadsheet), configure account prioritisation rules, build the recommended action engine, enforce a human approval gate, and set up outcome logging.

Question 03

Where does it run?

Inside your own cloud account (AWS, Hetzner, GCP, or private server) using your credentials and infrastructure. Data stays strictly within your security boundary — it never passes through shared third-party servers.

Question 04

What happens over 30 days?

We follow a clear 4-week execution timeline with weekly reviews and dedicated engineering support:

Week 01

Scope & Deploy

Define approval rules, select signal sources, and deploy baseline infrastructure.

Week 02

Connect & Build

Integrate 1–2 data sources, build prioritisation logic and action routing.

Week 03

Operate

Run live workflow with operator approval gate, process real signals.

Week 04

Measure & Review

Review outcome logs, deliver complete runbooks & transfer ownership.

Question 05

What do I own at the end?

You own 100% of the code, configuration files, workflow definitions, credentials, infrastructure access, runbooks, and documentation. There are zero recurring platform fees or lock-ins to Aethonex.
