Prospect Dominion Workflow Pilot

Initial test price: £2,500 UK / $3,000 US. These are initial test prices, not prices validated by completed customer purchases. The bounded scope, acceptance criteria, exclusions, and payment terms are confirmed in writing after feasibility review and before work begins.

Question 01

What workflow do we choose?

The initial target is a feasible outbound or GTM workflow that turns one supported, customer-provided CSV input into prioritized accounts and a human-approved next action. Up to 200 target accounts and five users, subject to feasibility.

Question 02

What gets built?

The bounded path uses one supported CSV input, one implemented prioritisation path, one human approval gate, and one already-supported and tested action provider or type. Documentation and handover are included. Provenance, action-result, and audit functions are included where supported; other connectors and action types are not implied.

Question 03

Where does it run?

The intended model is a customer-controlled, single-tenant environment. Hosting, external providers, data flows, and operational responsibilities must be confirmed during technical scoping. Do not provide customer data before those arrangements are approved.

Question 04

What happens over 30 days?

The illustrative 30-day schedule below starts only after access, security, provider, scope, and acceptance requirements are agreed:

Week 01

Scope & Deploy

Confirm the supported CSV input, approval rules, and deployment feasibility.

Week 02

Connect & Build

Import the agreed CSV signal file and configure deterministic prioritisation.

Week 03

Operate

Validate with approved data only after deployment and security gates pass.

Week 04

Measure & Review

Review outcome logs, deliver complete runbooks & transfer ownership.

Question 05

What do we retain?

The shared core is MIT-licensed. You retain your data, credentials, and infrastructure. Rights and handover for customer-specific work are defined in the written agreement.
