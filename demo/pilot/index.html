The 30-Day Pilot Breakdown

What is the proposed $4,500 pilot scope?

A proposed 30-day pilot for one signal-to-action workflow. Final technical fit, acceptance criteria, exclusions, and price must be agreed in writing before work begins.

Question 01

What workflow do we choose?

The initial target is an outbound agency workflow that turns a customer-provided signal export into prioritized accounts and human-approved next actions.

Question 02

What gets built?

The documented path imports one agreed CSV signal file, applies deterministic prioritisation, presents recommended actions for operator approval, and records outcomes. Additional source connectors are not included unless technically validated and agreed separately.

Question 03

Where does it run?

The intended model is a customer-controlled, single-tenant environment. Hosting, external providers, data flows, and operational responsibilities must be confirmed during technical scoping. Do not provide customer data before those arrangements are approved.

Question 04

What happens over 30 days?

The schedule below is illustrative and starts only after access, security, provider, and acceptance requirements are agreed:

Week 01

Scope & Deploy

Define approval rules, select signal sources, and deploy baseline infrastructure.

Week 02

Connect & Build

Import the agreed CSV signal file and configure deterministic prioritisation.

Week 03

Operate

Validate with approved data only after deployment and security gates pass.

Week 04

Measure & Review

Review outcome logs, deliver complete runbooks & transfer ownership.

Question 05

What do we retain?

The shared core is MIT-licensed. You retain your data, credentials, and infrastructure. Rights and handover for customer-specific work are defined in the written agreement.
