Scope and costs

One defined pilot; everything else is scoped separately.

The core is MIT-licensed; the paid service is implementation around it, not exclusive access to the source. You retain your data, credentials, and infrastructure; rights to custom work are defined in the written agreement. Hosting, external services, and work beyond the pilot scope are not included unless explicitly stated.

The initial test price is not validated by completed customer purchases. After feasibility review, a written proposal confirms the final scope, acceptance criteria, any separately priced costs, and payment terms before work is authorized. The Pilot does not include ongoing care or hosting. Workflow Care & Improvement is a separate Aethonex service at £750 UK / $950 US per month, available only after successful deployment and where appropriate. It includes up to four defined hours of maintenance or small changes and one operational review; hosting, third-party costs, major development, and 24/7 support are excluded. See the current Aethonex service terms.

What the pilot does not promise

A working system, not a revenue guarantee.

The pilot measures system performance: time from signal to approved action, acted-on signals, approval decisions, operator time saved, and attributable downstream activity. No software lock-in — you keep the deployment and documentation.
