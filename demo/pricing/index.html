Scope and costs

One defined pilot; everything else is scoped separately.

The core is MIT-licensed. You retain your data, credentials, and infrastructure; rights to custom work are defined in the written agreement. Hosting, external services, and work beyond the pilot scope are not included unless explicitly stated.

No standard post-pilot deployment tier or partner program is currently offered.

What the pilot does not promise

A working system, not a revenue guarantee.

The pilot measures system performance: time from signal to approved action, acted-on signals, approval decisions, operator time saved, and attributable downstream activity. No software lock-in — you keep the deployment and documentation.
