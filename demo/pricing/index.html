The proposed offer is a bounded pilot for one signal-to-action workflow. No fixed pilot price has been validated or published. Technical fit, scope, acceptance criteria, costs, and terms must be agreed in writing before any engagement.

Proposed Dominion Pilot

Scoped proposal

Up to 30 days · one workflow · subject to technical fit

Proposed scope: deploy the baseline, import one agreed CSV signal export, configure prioritisation, add a human approval step, log outcomes, and hand over documentation. Other connectors require technical validation and separate written scope.

  • Dominion deployment baseline
  • One customer-provided CSV signal input
  • One signal-to-action workflow
  • Account prioritisation logic
  • Recommended action & human approval gate
  • Execution handoff & outcome logging
  • Review and handover, subject to agreed acceptance criteria
  • Documentation & complete runbooks

Scope and costs

One defined pilot; everything else is scoped separately.

The core is MIT-licensed. You retain your data, credentials, and infrastructure; rights to custom work are defined in the written agreement. Hosting, external services, and work beyond the pilot scope are not included unless explicitly stated.

No fixed rate, standard post-pilot deployment tier, or partner program is currently published. Any proposal must state the price and payment terms before work is authorized.

What the pilot does not promise

A working system, not a revenue guarantee.

The pilot measures system performance: time from signal to approved action, acted-on signals, approval decisions, operator time saved, and attributable downstream activity. No software lock-in — you keep the deployment and documentation.
