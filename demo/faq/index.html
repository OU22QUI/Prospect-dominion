Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you book a walkthrough.

What is Prospect Dominion?

It is an owned, governed signal-to-action layer deployed in your environment. It connects selected signals to prioritised accounts, context, a recommended next action, and a human approval step.

Who is Aethonex?

Aethonex is a UK-based software project developing Prospect Dominion and related automation software. It is pre-revenue and pre-incorporation; UK incorporation is planned.

Is the public demo live?

No. The public demo is a simulation using fictional sample accounts, seeded activity, and illustrative values. It sends no external actions and does not use customer data.

What does the Prospect Dominion Workflow Pilot include?

A 30-day initial paid design-partner engagement, subject to feasibility. Initial test price: £2,500 UK / $3,000 US; these prices are not validated by completed customer purchases. Scope is one bounded workflow in a customer-controlled environment, one supported CSV input, one implemented prioritisation path, one human approval gate, and one already-supported and tested action provider or type. Up to 200 target accounts and five users, subject to feasibility, with documentation, handover, agreed acceptance criteria, and provenance, action-result, or audit functions where supported. Final scope and payment terms are confirmed in writing after review.

What does the published pilot price cover?

It is an initial test price for the feasibility-dependent pilot scope above, not a price validated by completed customer purchases. Hosting, third-party costs, and work beyond the agreed scope are excluded unless separately agreed in writing. A free 15–20 minute Workflow Fit Call is a qualification conversation, not a free technical audit or implementation. Workflow Care & Improvement is separate at £750 UK / $950 US per month, available only after successful deployment and where ongoing support is appropriate; it includes up to four defined hours and one operational review, with hosting, third-party costs, major development, and 24/7 support excluded. See current Aethonex service terms.

Does Dominion replace our CRM or sequencer?

No. The pilot is scoped to one bounded workflow and a supported, tested action path; it does not replace a CRM or sequencer. Provider compatibility must be confirmed before scope is agreed.

Is it autonomous?

No. The commercial model is governed: a human operator approves, edits, or rejects an external action before it is sent.

Where does it run?

The intended deployment is customer-controlled and single-tenant. Hosting, external providers, data flows, and operational responsibilities are confirmed during technical scoping; do not provide customer data before those arrangements are approved.

What do we retain?

The shared core is MIT-licensed. You retain your data, credentials, and infrastructure. Rights and handover for customer-specific work are defined in the written agreement.
