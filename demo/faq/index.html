Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you book a walkthrough.

What is Prospect Dominion?

It is an owned, governed signal-to-action layer deployed in your environment. It connects selected signals to prioritised accounts, context, a recommended next action, and a human approval step.

Is the public demo live?

No. The public demo is a simulation using fictional sample accounts, seeded activity, and illustrative values. It sends no external actions and does not use customer data.

What does the pilot include?

A 30-day, fixed-scope build for one workflow: deployment baseline, 1–2 sources, up to 200 target accounts, prioritisation logic, recommended-action logic, human approval, outcome logging, and handover documentation.

What does the pilot cost?

The Dominion Pilot is $4,500 fixed. Deployment starts at $15,000 setup plus $1,500/month for operate and extend.

Does Dominion replace our CRM or sequencer?

No. Dominion sits between the tools you keep. It is designed to connect a workflow across your existing stack, not replace the systems that already own your records or sending.

Is it autonomous?

No. The commercial model is governed: a person approves, edits, or rejects an external action before it is sent.

Where does it run?

The deployment is single-tenant and is preferred in your cloud or VPS. A temporary hosted sandbox can be used for a pilot when your environment is not ready, with migration to your environment afterward.

What is real today?

The deployment baseline includes the FastAPI service, accounts, people, threads, events, outcomes, workflow state machine, authentication, readiness, branding, and deployment verification. Signal ingestion, prioritisation rules, relationship context, and integrations are built for the chosen pilot workflow.

What happens after the pilot?

You receive a working system, baseline-vs-pilot measurements, and handover documentation. You can expand to deployment, keep the pilot as-is, or stop. No revenue outcome is guaranteed.
