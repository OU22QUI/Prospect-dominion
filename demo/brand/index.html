Prospect Dominion Logo

Aethonex product mark

Prospect Dominion

A steel D in two pieces, with one gold arrowhead pointing out of it. The frame is the structure an agency runs on. The gold arrow is the outbound it owns.

The idea

Same grammar as Aethonex, a different direction

Both marks are cut steel with a single gold triangle in a triangular opening. Gold is owned and grey is rented, as in the brand brief. In the Aethonex mark the triangle points up, at the centre of what you own. In Prospect Dominion it points out, at the people you contact.

D for Dominion

The letter reads at once and fills a square, so it works as an app icon and an avatar.

Two pieces, one gap

The stem is cut away from the bowl, like the separate steel pieces in the Aethonex mark. It shows a product built from parts the agency controls.

An arrowhead, not a play button

The base of the gold shape is notched, so it reads as a pointer sent outward. A plain triangle is used only in the favicon.

Versions

Every version, ready to use

The wordmark is Syne Bold, outlined, set in capitals. Both words have eight letters and are spaced to the same width, so they stack as a block. The endorsement line is Inter Medium. Use the endorsed lockup where Aethonex needs to be named, such as the app footer and sales material. Use the plain lockup inside the product.

Construction

Built on a 1,000-unit grid

  • Clear space equals the width of the stem, 160 units, on every side. Nothing enters it.
  • The gap between stem and bowl is 30 units. Keep it open in every version, including one colour.
  • The arrow sits 36 units inside the opening on every side. It never touches the steel.
  • Proportion is 665 wide by 700 high. The mark is nearly square, so it centres in a square icon with equal margins.

Rules

Keep it recognisable

  • Do keep the arrow goldIt is the one owned thing, as in every Aethonex asset.
  • Do use dark grounds firstThe mark lives on Ground, Card or Panel. Light is for print.
  • Do name it in fullWrite Prospect Dominion. The endorsement line reads "by Aethonex".
  • Don't recolourNo blue, no gold stem, no silver arrow.
  • Don't add effectsNo bevel, glow, gradient or shadow on the mark. Only transparency from palette colours is allowed.
  • Don't close the gapThe stem and the bowl stay separate.
  • Don't merge with Nex or the Aethonex markEach stands alone. Nex never appears inside the product mark.
  • Don't shorten or extend the nameNever Dominion Suite, Dominion Cloud or PD.
  • Don't place it on gold or on photosThe arrow disappears.
